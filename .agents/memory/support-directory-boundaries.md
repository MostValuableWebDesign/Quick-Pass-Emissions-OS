---
name: Support contact trust boundaries
description: Why source-only operational contacts and verified work assignments have distinct eligibility.
---

Staff may use honestly labeled document-sourced contacts, but importing an old guide never attests that the contact was confirmed or authorizes a new verified work assignment.

**Why:** The business request explicitly permits useful operational contact information while preserving existing vendor verification requirements. Incomplete personal names and retailer mentions are evidence for management review, not identities to invent.

**How to apply:** Keep confirmation dates unknown until a manager actually checks the contact. Preserve the verified-vendor assignment boundary separately from directory visibility and self-reported communication. Do not infer physical equipment assignments or preferred choices from source wording.
