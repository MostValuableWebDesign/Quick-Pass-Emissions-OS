---
name: Personal Notes & Tasks scope
description: The user's personal workspace is separate from staff duties and business approvals.
---

Notes & Tasks is a private workspace for the signed-in person: no team features, assignments, accountability tracking, approval workflows, or new access-management screens.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a personal place to capture business ideas and review accomplishments, not another operational task-management system.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing login; keep each account's content private. Biweekly summaries use recorded completion/reopening history and America/New_York calendar dates. Never infer accomplishments or hours worked. Do not represent page visits or a manual trigger as unattended scheduling.

Expense tracking extends this lightweight personal workspace; it is recordkeeping, not authorization to move money or revive the retired Operational Finance section. Leave tax deductibility decisions to the owner and accountant.

**Why:** The user requested quick business-spending records linked to notes/tasks, separated planned purchases from actual spending, and explicitly reserved tax decisions for themselves and their accountant.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing billing and payroll boundaries. Keep optional details and receipts fillable later; missing details should be gentle prompts, not purchase-approval requirements.
