IMPLEMENT

1. Owner overview and scheduled summaries
Provide an owner-only overview combining:
- Overdue responsibilities and pending reviews.
- Upcoming/overdue maintenance and unresolved downtime.
- Employee certification expirations.
- Certificate and chemical inventory warnings.
- Payroll items awaiting review.
- Outstanding dealership invoices and rental balances.

Let the owner configure daily/weekly summary schedules in America/New_York. Save generated summaries with their generation time, reporting period and links to underlying records. Clearly distinguish a saved historical summary from current live figures.

2. Actionable reminders
Add configurable advance reminders and overdue escalation for maintenance, employee certification expiry, unpaid dealership invoices and rental charges.

Assign reminders to authorized internal recipients. Support acknowledgement and resolution. Resolve or supersede reminders when the underlying condition changes; acknowledgement must not falsely mark an invoice paid or maintenance completed.

Reuse existing task and notification behavior without creating duplicate alerts.

3. Inventory forecasts
Extend certificate forecasts and add chemical depletion estimates using each item's own recorded consumption and units.

Show the observation window, usable data coverage, average consumption and estimated depletion date. Label forecasts as estimates. Handle unknown opening stock, insufficient history, zero usage, reconciliation adjustments and voids correctly. Do not count receipts or reconciliation adjustments as consumption. Do not convert incompatible chemical units.

Allow owner-configured thresholds and planning lead times. Never automatically place orders.

4. Reporting and trends
Add useful date-filtered reports for:
- Responsibility completion and overdue work.
- Bay downtime and maintenance activity/costs.
- Certificate and chemical consumption.
- Dealership billing, receipts and receivables aging.
- Rental charges, receipts and outstanding balances.
- Finance records separated by manual, independently verified and estimated status.

Use existing domain calculations and reversal/void rules. Distinguish invoice issue dates, payment dates and service dates. Explain date semantics and missing-data limits. Do not label entered records as reconciled profit or complete accounting books.

Include authorized CSV exports for complete filtered results, not just the latest 100 displayed rows. Preserve exact cents and protect against CSV formula injection.

5. Reliable scheduling
Use the existing independently triggered operations scheduler. Add durable job/delivery records, duplicate prevention, transaction-safe locking, bounded retries and observable failures as needed.

Repeated or concurrent execution must not duplicate summaries, reminders or notifications. Handle missed runs, closures where applicable, daylight saving changes and schedule edits explicitly.

A browser timer or page visit must never be required for automation to run.

6. Permissions and notification channels
Enforce every read, export and mutation on the server using current account roles and active status.

Owners receive the consolidated business summary. Managers/bookkeepers receive only information allowed by their existing permissions. Employees must never receive peer wages, financial reports, private management notes or privileged summary links.

Implement in-app notifications first. External email/SMS is optional and must remain disabled until a provider, credentials, verified recipients and explicit sending authorization are configured. Do not send customer/dealership messages during development.
