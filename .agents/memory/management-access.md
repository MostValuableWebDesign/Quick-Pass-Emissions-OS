name: Management-only access
description: Owner-approved sign-in policy and staff-history preservation boundary.
---

Keep Quick Pass sign-in management-only: owners and admins can sign in; employee and bookkeeper identities remain business records, not active login accounts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose "Keep management-only access" after being told the incoming migration deletes non-management login credentials and sessions while preserving staff, payroll and business history.

**How to apply:** Do not restore non-management login access when reconciling older navigation, authentication or fixture code. Preserve staff identities and linked operational/payroll records; retiring login access is not permission to delete business history.

Employee contact creation must not ask for an email or app-access setting. The employee form's “Role” means a job role, such as Inspector, not a sign-in permission.

**Why:** The user asked to remove email and App access from employee creation while adding contact details and Inspector roles.

**How to apply:** Keep staff contacts separate from authentication administration. Preserve existing account identities and history during contact edits; never promote a staff record based on its job-role text.

Account removal in Settings means deactivating other users, not deleting their records. Owners must not deactivate their own account.

**Why:** The user chose “Deactivate other users” when asked which account-removal scope to enable.

**How to apply:** Preserve linked business history, revoke sessions and pending invitations, and offer reactivation. Keep owner-only user creation and sign-in privileges separate from employee job roles.
