---
name: Marketing scope
description: Permission and outbound-action boundaries for future Marketing work.
---

Marketing is a recordkeeping and evidence-reporting feature, not permission to send promotions, buy ads, post social content or publish Google review replies.

**Why:** The user explicitly scoped Marketing to recorded campaigns, actual results and owner-controlled drafts, with no outbound marketing or publication.

**How to apply:** Keep any future Google integration read-only for reporting and feedback analysis. Reply drafts require authorized dated review evidence and must not be published automatically. Do not invent account access, review data or customer attribution.

Promotions must integrate with an existing pricing/payment workflow rather than creating a parallel payment system or silently rewriting historical charges.

**Why:** The user required preservation of historical terms and existing billing/payment behavior.

**How to apply:** New checkout support or zero-charge offers require an explicit extension of the real billing workflow. Keep recorded collections distinct from Performance estimates and unavailable data explicit.
