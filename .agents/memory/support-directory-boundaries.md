---
name: Support contact trust boundaries
description: Why source-only operational contacts and verified work assignments have distinct eligibility.
---

Staff may use honestly labeled document-sourced contacts, but importing an old guide never attests that the contact was confirmed or authorizes a new verified work assignment.

**Why:** The business request explicitly permits useful operational contact information while preserving existing vendor verification requirements. Incomplete personal names and retailer mentions are evidence for management review, not identities to invent.

**How to apply:** Keep confirmation dates unknown until a manager actually checks the contact. Preserve the verified-vendor assignment boundary separately from directory visibility and self-reported communication. Do not infer physical equipment assignments or preferred choices from source wording.

Verify contact persistence in the identity-pinned database used by the running app before telling the owner the contacts are available. Source preparation, import documentation and results from another environment are not proof of current saved records.

**Why:** The live directory was empty despite import notes reporting that contacts had been applied. Reading the active store exposed the mismatch.

**How to apply:** Verify the supplied fields after saving, read them through the normal authorized directory service, and check that a repeated import would not create duplicates. Keep corrections additive and preserve existing records.

An explicitly owner-requested named contact can be displayed as unconfirmed even if no phone number was supplied. Display only the supplied name and clearly show missing contact details; never invent a surname or business identity.

**Why:** A useful camera-support contact was hidden in review instead of appearing with the contacts the owner requested.

**How to apply:** Keep verification dates and assignment eligibility separate from contact visibility. This does not turn every incomplete name in an imported guide into a verified provider.

The owner specifies KleenRite for hoses, guns, nozzles, brushes, carwash parts and cleaning supplies. The separate responsibility to stock foam soap, tire cleaner, high-pressure soap and clear wax does not assign those chemicals to KleenRite.

**Why:** The owner clarified KleenRite's supply scope while keeping general chemical-stock duties distinct.

**How to apply:** Show KleenRite under Wash Parts Supply with the stated capabilities. Do not infer that KleenRite supplies the listed wash chemicals.
