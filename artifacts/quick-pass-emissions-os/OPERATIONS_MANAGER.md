# Operations Manager workspace

Open **Overview → Operations manager** (`/operations`). Owners and managers use the same operational workspace. Staff never log in. PostgreSQL retains tasks, schedules, stock records, transaction evidence and audit history across builds using the existing persistent database. Migration `202610170002_manager_workspace` is additive; it does not seed business records, change pay rules, provision a scheduler or post financial transactions.

## Overview and ownership

The overview joins existing responsibility records, shifts, equipment/bays, certificate/chemical forecasts, payroll drafts, dealership/rental receivables, maintenance/downtime and marketing campaigns. Links open the original workflows. Tasks have 15-item pages with active, overdue, review and completed filters and separate business areas: emissions, tag renewal, self-service wash, detail rentals, property, payroll, billing, marketing, website and general. Source summaries are bounded and explicitly link to full registers; they are not complete accounting books.

Missing shifts do not mean no staff; no reported outage does not confirm availability. Inspections are not collected revenue. Estimated depletion is not a verified usage report. Approved gross payroll is not paid payroll. Payment evidence is separately recorded and independently reconciled. Existing Dashboard, Performance, operational reports and financial reports keep their source definitions.

Confirmed reference hours supplied by the owner are Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM, Saturday 8 AM–5 PM, Sunday closed, for emissions and the kiosk. These do not set personal availability or carwash hours. The six-bay layout remains in the existing Carwash register: three self-service and three rented detail bays. Rental amounts, dates and collection/tenant terms require the existing owner authorization; this workspace generates no rental charges.

## Responsibilities, recurrence and review

The existing `TaskTemplate`, `TaskInstance`, `TaskEvent` and audit records are extended rather than replaced. A responsibility records execution assignee, management oversight, business area, optional station/property label, priority, deadline, estimated minutes, checklist, dependencies, notes, optional evidence/reference and status. To do and Waiting display the existing PENDING and BLOCKED storage states. Management records work performed by staff; delegation does not create staff accounts or self-service access.

Checklists and prerequisites must be finished before completion. Required prerequisite reviews must also be complete. Cycles and self-dependencies are rejected. A manager-workspace completion requiring review must be reviewed by a different management account from the person who recorded completion. Reopening clears completion/review timestamps and preserves events/audits. Versioned updates reject stale edits. Completing a task does not submit ACH, receive supplies, approve/pay payroll, confirm deposits, repair equipment or publish marketing/website work.

One-time projects generate their original occurrence immediately. Daily duties use selected weekdays (or existing explicitly configured operating days); weekly duties require weekdays. Monthly/quarterly/annual duties use the existing anchored recurrence rules. Configured closures remain respected. The unique template/date constraint prevents duplicate generation. Template edits affect new occurrences only; existing snapshots and approved schedules remain intact. Cancel or edit overlapping occurrences explicitly. Pausing/resuming is versioned; resuming skips missed dates during the pause, while already generated tasks remain for review.

**Access-driven scheduling:** opening `/operations` generates manager occurrences and inventory/problem follow-ups in a short authorized database transaction. This is the requested explicit exception to the older read-only page-generation convention. It makes no provider calls and runs no unrelated automation. A persistent per-template cursor catches up missed dates, at most 93 calendar days per duty per access, through 14 days ahead. A catch-up notice and Continue button remain when needed. It never discards an extended backlog simply because it is older than 31 days. Newly entered start dates are bounded to the last 31 days or later. Template/date uniqueness and the shared lock make simultaneous opens idempotent.

The existing independent operations job still handles its original recurring duties and reminders; manager templates are excluded from that generator so it cannot create incomplete manager snapshots. Its existing overdue-notification reads can see persisted manager tasks. No new worker, subscription, Replit Scheduled Deployment or browser timer exists. Manager occurrence/trigger generation while nobody opens the workspace is **not configured**. Reliable unattended reminders still require the existing independently provisioned runner; no live runner was provisioned or verified here.

Low recorded certificates/chemicals use existing owner policies. General supplies require a known count and configured reorder level. Each source has one open follow-up (including completion awaiting required review); repeated checks, multiple managers and repeat visits do not duplicate it. A new same-day occurrence is also prevented by template/date uniqueness. Maintenance issues link to their original tickets; critical or camera/security issues receive urgent follow-ups. Resolution remains in the source workflow, with task review separate.

## Personal schedule

**My weekly schedule** stores each signed-in manager's own working days, hours, breaks and interruption reserve. The initial 09:00/17:00 and 20% control values are editable suggestions, not saved availability: all days start disabled. Break syntax is `12:00-12:30,15:00-15:15`. Add dated fixed commitments separately; cancellations retain audit history.

Normal account provisioning already includes a responsibility profile. If an older management account lacks one, the workspace shows an explicit Create my responsibility profile action. It creates only that account's profile under current management authorization; no pay arrangement or login role is added. The profile may appear as unconfigured in payroll setup. Source-trigger assignment and personal schedule approval remain unavailable until it exists.

Until working days are configured, previews remain drafts with work explicitly unscheduled. A deterministic seven-day proposal places only responsibilities assigned to the manager's staff profile, within saved availability, outside breaks/commitments and before their original deadlines. Critical/high work comes first, with prerequisites before dependent tasks. Reserve capacity is removed from the end of available daily capacity. Overdue, blocked or excessive work remains visible as unscheduled; deadlines and employee shifts are never silently changed. Eastern time and DST checks prevent invalid local clock slots and duration distortions.

Review conflicts and unscheduled work, then approve. Approval rechecks availability revision, task versions, current time and all commitments after acquiring locks; stale proposals require a new preview. Overlapping older approved weeks are superseded, not deleted. Approved plans are retained snapshots; subsequent task changes need a new preview and approval. Fixed-commitment conflicts prevent approval. Unscheduled work can remain explicitly listed in an approved plan.

The optional editable starter duties include opening/service readiness, closing, the owner-supplied Monday–Saturday focus, four payroll stages, website checks and marketing/community review. Suggested durations/deadlines are planning estimates and must be edited for real availability. No licensing or introductory inspection/equipment/kiosk training tasks are created. Repeating the starter action does not duplicate its templates.

## Payroll and deposits

Monday is payday; the existing yellow/orange/red reminder and daily acknowledgement remain unchanged. Starter duties separate preparation, review, approval and payment confirmation. All rates, eligibility, source approvals, effective dates, frozen drafts, commission math and independent payroll approval remain in the existing Payroll & commissions module.

The pay-guide disclosure is reference only: Al $1,150/week ($23 × 10 hours × 5 days), John $1,150/week, Clinton approximately $900/week with variable hours, Sabino $100/Monday plus $50 for grass cutting when performed. The guide's $1 above 50 daily vehicles per eligible employee can conflict with existing personal attribution policies and Performance's shop estimate. Verify eligibility, scope, attendance, free retests, periods and guaranteed pay. No named staff matching or pay-rule overwrite occurs. **Separate grass-cutting compensation rules/payment creation remain unconfigured**; verified compensation must appear once in approved payroll, not as a payment from a task or an assumed $50 supplement.

Payments & deposits is an internal **evidence register**, not a payment/bank/expense system. An owner may attach external payment evidence matching an independently approved gross draft. The approved payroll period is unique across this register; a revised draft cannot produce another confirmation for the same period. References are unique. Gross-payment evidence is not proof of net/tax settlement; record the breakdown in notes and reconcile against external payroll evidence. A second owner independently reconciles the record. No bank transfer, duplicate financial receipt, tax calculation or payroll recalculation occurs.

Checks awaiting deposit have a unique check/deposit identifier. Submission records the same record's REMOTE/BANK method, chronological date and external reference; its previous check reference remains in audit. Remote deposits require a bank-authorized maximum entered by an owner. No large-deposit threshold is invented. A check cannot be bank-reconciled until external deposit submission is recorded. The recording account cannot independently reconcile its own evidence. The register has 15-item pages; missing records never mean a confirmed zero bank balance. It does not alter dealership receipts, rentals, revenue reports or cash balances. Existing invoice drafts and billing terms remain configurable in their source modules; no automatic customer emailing is introduced.
