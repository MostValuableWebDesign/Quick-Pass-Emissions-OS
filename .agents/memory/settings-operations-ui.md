---
name: Settings and operations UI scope
description: User-defined locations and removed panels for Quick Pass settings and navigation.
---

Station reporting configuration belongs in the general Settings area and must remain visibly distinct from Business operating days.

**Why:** The user asked to move Station settings out of Performance and avoid overlapping the separate Business operating days settings.

**How to apply:** Keep station performance hours and projection controls separate from business schedule flags, label their scope clearly, and preserve existing owner-only authorization.

Account & password belongs beneath Settings, and pay setup belongs in Payroll rather than Performance. Group Settings by related business operations, Emissions configuration, and activity.

**Why:** The user requested these locations and a better-organized Settings area.

**How to apply:** Keep the Settings label concise, use nested account navigation, and retain extra spacing below the Settings navigation area. Retire the Performance setup screen without deleting saved data or station-import capabilities.

Reminders and Notifications belong under Settings. Settings and Dealerships use nested navigation that opens on hover, with categorized Settings functions; Invoices and Payments belong under Dealerships.

**Why:** The user requested hover-revealed nesting and explicitly rejected an uncategorized Settings list.

**How to apply:** Preserve these navigation groups, role restrictions, and keyboard/touch access when adding related pages. Do not return these tools to the top-level sidebar.

The user removed the Issued By mappings, Outcome rules & prices, Operations scheduler, Product roadmap, Data storage & recovery, and sidebar slogan panels as unnecessary.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested those visible panels be removed.

**How to apply:** Do not restore these panels without asking. Removing their UI does not authorize deleting persisted configuration or the calculations that use it.
